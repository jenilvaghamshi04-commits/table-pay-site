A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear
things up.

Something else on your mind?

Can I try Salon Desk before choosing a plan?+

Yes. Open the interactive workspace to explore the features, add test visits, and try the daily workflow. It runs in your browser without login. Use sample details rather than real customer information while testing.

Where are my records stored?+

The current workspace saves records in the browser and device where you use it. Records do not automatically sync between devices. Clearing browser storage deletes them, so use Export backup regularly. Managed cloud storage would require a separate setup and agreement.

Does it send WhatsApp invoices automatically?+

It prepares a WhatsApp message with the invoice details and a shareable invoice link. You review the message and press Send in WhatsApp. There is no automatic WhatsApp Business API messaging.

Can I use my own salon name and colours?+

Yes. Brand settings include your salon name, contact details, logo URL, invoice footer, and colours. Saved invoices retain their brand snapshot. You can preview a few colour directions above.

Are payments and subscriptions collected on this website?+

No. This website presents the product and proposed pricing. It does not process payments or start subscriptions. Use the enquiry button to discuss your salon and confirm the scope before purchasing.

Can I manage multiple salons with one account?+

The current no-login workspace is for one browser workspace. Central admin accounts, salon access controls, shared records, and multi-location cloud management are not included in this version.
